One hour a week

This is the hour the whole department is in the same room. Every laboratory hosts, the speaker is an ambassador for the host lab, and the talk is pitched to a general microbiology audience — polished work or work in progress, both welcome.

This is a living schedule. It changes as slots are traded, speakers are confirmed and defenses are booked. Whatever is on this page is current; if something here is wrong or out of date, tell me and it gets fixed the same day.

Book your defense into this hour

If you are defending this year, use one of these standing slots for the public part of your defense. The room is booked, the department is already coming, and you get the audience your work deserves rather than whoever happens to be free at 3pm on a Tuesday.

Students and advisors: pick a date below that is open or still TBD, and coordinate it through the department. A defense takes priority over a TBD slot — that is what the TBD is for.

The empty slots are an invitation

Slots marked open have no host yet, and slots marked needs speaker have a host but no name. Either is a place to bring in an outside speaker you have wanted to hear from, or to give someone in your lab the floor. You do not need to wait to be asked.
